## 摘要 / Abstract

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Despite great progress in simulating multiphysics problems using the numerical discretization of partial differential equations (PDEs), one still cannot seamlessly incorporate noisy data into existing algorithms, mesh generation remains complex, and high-dimensional problems governed by parameterized PDEs cannot be tackled. Moreover, solving inverse problems with hidden physics is often prohibitively expensive and requires different formulations and elaborate computer codes. | 尽管在用偏微分方程（PDE）的数值离散来模拟多物理问题方面已取得巨大进展，人们仍无法把带噪数据无缝地纳入现有算法，网格生成依然复杂，而由参数化 PDE 支配的高维问题则无法处理。此外，求解具有隐藏物理的逆问题往往代价高得无法承受，且需要不同的表述形式与复杂的计算机代码。 |
| Machine learning has emerged as a promising alternative, but training deep neural networks requires big data, not always available for scientific problems. Instead, such networks can be trained from additional information obtained by enforcing the physical laws (for example, at random points in the continuous space-time domain). | 机器学习已成为一种有前景的替代方案，但训练深度神经网络需要大数据，而科学问题并不总能获得大数据。取而代之，这类网络可以从施加物理定律所获得的额外信息中接受训练（例如，在连续时空域中的随机点上施加物理定律）。 |
| Such physics-informed learning integrates (noisy) data and mathematical models, and implements them through neural networks or other kernel-based regression networks. Moreover, it may be possible to design specialized network architectures that automatically satisfy some of the physical invariants for better accuracy, faster training and improved generalization. | 这种物理信息学习整合了（带噪）数据与数学模型，并通过神经网络或其他基于核的回归网络来实现。此外，还有可能设计出能自动满足某些物理不变量的专门网络架构，以获得更高的精度、更快的训练和更好的泛化。 |
| Here, we review some of the prevailing trends in embedding physics into machine learning, present some of the current capabilities and limitations and discuss diverse applications of physics-informed learning both for forward and inverse problems, including discovering hidden physics and tackling high-dimensional problems. | 在此，我们回顾把物理嵌入机器学习的一些主流趋势，介绍当前的一些能力与局限，并讨论物理信息学习在正问题与逆问题中的多样应用，包括发现隐藏物理与处理高维问题。 |

## 引言（开篇）/ Introduction

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Modelling and forecasting the dynamics of multiphysics and multiscale systems remains an open scientific problem. Take for instance the Earth system, a uniquely complex system whose dynamics are intricately governed by the interaction of physical, chemical and biological processes taking place on spatiotemporal scales that span 17 orders of magnitude^1^. In the past 50 years, there has been tremendous progress in understanding multiscale physics in diverse applications, from geophysics to biophysics, by numerically solving partial differential equations (PDEs) using finite differences, finite elements, spectral and even meshless methods. Despite relentless progress, modelling and predicting the evolution of nonlinear multiscale systems with inhomogeneous cascades-of-scales by using classical analytical or computational tools inevitably faces severe challenges and introduces prohibitive cost and multiple sources of uncertainty. Moreover, solving inverse problems (for inferring material properties in functional materials or discovering missing physics in reactive transport, for example) is often prohibitively expensive and requires complex formulations, new algorithms and elaborate computer codes. Most importantly, solving real-life physical problems with missing, gappy or noisy boundary conditions through traditional approaches is currently impossible. | 对多物理、多尺度系统动力学进行建模与预测仍是一个未解的科学问题。以地球系统为例，它是一个格外复杂的系统，其动力学由物理、化学和生物过程在跨越 **17 个数量级**的时空尺度上的相互作用精巧地支配^1^。过去 50 年间，人们通过用有限差分、有限元、谱方法乃至无网格方法数值求解偏微分方程（PDE），在地球物理到生物物理等众多应用中理解多尺度物理方面取得了巨大进展。尽管进步不断，用经典解析或计算工具来建模并预测具有非均匀尺度级串（inhomogeneous cascades-of-scales）的非线性多尺度系统的演化，仍不可避免地面临严峻挑战，并带来高得无法承受的成本与多种不确定性来源。此外，求解逆问题（例如推断功能材料的材料属性，或发现反应输运中缺失的物理）往往昂贵得难以承受，且需要复杂的表述形式、新的算法与精心编写的计算机代码。最重要的是，**用传统方法求解边界条件缺失、有缺口或带噪的真实物理问题，目前是不可能的。** |
| This is where and why observational data play a crucial role. With the prospect of more than a trillion sensors in the next decade, including airborne, seaborne and satellite remote sensing, a wealth of multi-fidelity observations is ready to be explored through data-driven methods. However, despite the volume, velocity and variety of available (collected or generated) data streams, in many real cases it is still not possible to seamlessly incorporate such multi-fidelity data into existing physical models. Mathematical (and practical) data-assimilation efforts have been blossoming; yet the wealth and the spatiotemporal heterogeneity of available data, along with the lack of universally acceptable models, underscores the need for a transformative approach. | 这正是观测数据发挥关键作用之所在、也是其原因。展望未来十年将有超过一万亿个传感器（包括机载、船载与卫星遥感），大量多保真度观测数据正有待通过数据驱动方法加以挖掘。然而，尽管可获得（采集或生成）的数据流在体量、速度与多样性上都极为丰富，在许多真实情形中仍无法把这类多保真度数据无缝地纳入现有物理模型。数学上（及实践上）的数据同化努力欣欣向荣；但可用数据的丰富性与时空异质性，加上缺乏普遍可接受的模型，凸显出对一种变革性方法的需求。 |
| This is where machine learning (ML) has come into play. It can explore massive design spaces, identify multi-dimensional correlations and manage ill-posed problems. It can, for instance, help to detect climate extremes or statistically predict dynamic variables such as precipitation or vegetation productivity^2,3^. Deep learning approaches, in particular, naturally provide tools for automatically extracting features from massive amounts of multi-fidelity observational data that are currently available and characterized by unprecedented spatial and temporal coverage^4^. They can also help to link these features with existing approximate models and exploit them in building new predictive tools. Even for biophysical and biomedical modelling, this synergistic integration between ML tools and multiscale and multiphysics models has been recently advocated^5^. | 这正是机器学习（ML）发挥作用的场合。它能够探索巨大的设计空间、识别多维关联并处理病态问题。例如，它可以帮助检测极端气候事件，或统计预测降水、植被生产力等动态变量^2,3^。尤其是深度学习方法，天然提供了从当前可得、且具有前所未有的时空覆盖度的大量多保真度观测数据中自动提取特征的工具^4^。它们还能帮助把这些特征与现有近似模型联系起来，并在构建新的预测工具时加以利用。即便对于生物物理与生物医学建模，ML 工具与多尺度多物理模型之间的这种协同整合近来也已被倡导^5^。 |

## 为什么需要物理信息学习 / Motivation

**数据洪流与物理不一致（P2 第 1 段）**

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| A common current theme across scientific domains is that the ability to collect and create observational data far outpaces the ability to assimilate it sensibly, let alone understand it^4^ (Box 1). Despite their towering empirical promise and some preliminary success^6^, most ML approaches currently are unable to extract interpretable information and knowledge from this data deluge. Moreover, purely data-driven models may fit observations very well, but predictions may be physically inconsistent or implausible, owing to extrapolation or observational biases that may lead to poor generalization performance. Therefore, there is a pressing need for integrating fundamental physical laws and domain knowledge by 'teaching' ML models about governing physical rules, which can, in turn, provide 'informative priors' — that is, strong theoretical constraints and inductive biases on top of the observational ones. To this end, physics-informed learning is needed, hereby defined as the process by which prior knowledge stemming from our observational, empirical, physical or mathematical understanding of the world can be leveraged to improve the performance of a learning algorithm. | 当前横跨各科学领域的一个共同主题是：**收集与创造观测数据的能力远远超过了合理地同化这些数据的能力，更不用说理解它们了**^4^（Box 1）。尽管机器学习展现出极高的经验前景并已取得一些初步成功^6^，目前大多数 ML 方法仍无法从这股数据洪流中提取可解释的信息与知识。此外，纯数据驱动模型可能对观测拟合得非常好，但其预测却可能在物理上不一致或不可信——这是外推或观测偏差导致泛化性能不佳所致。因此，迫切需要通过"教会" ML 模型支配性的物理规则，把基本物理定律与领域知识整合进来；这反过来又能提供"**信息性先验（informative priors）**"——即在观测性偏置之上再叠加**强理论约束与归纳偏置**。为此，需要**物理信息学习**，其定义如下：**利用源自我们对世界的观测性、经验性、物理性或数学性理解所得的先验知识，来提升学习算法性能的过程。** |

**物理信息神经网络（PINNs）的定位（P2 第 2 段）**

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| A recent example reflecting this new learning philosophy is the family of 'physics-informed neural networks' (PINNs)^7^. This is a class of deep learning algorithms that can seamlessly integrate data and abstract mathematical operators, including PDEs with or without missing physics (Boxes 2,3). The leading motivation for developing these algorithms is that such prior knowledge or constraints can yield more interpretable ML methods that remain robust in the presence of imperfect data (such as missing or noisy values, outliers and so on) and can provide accurate and physically consistent predictions, even for extrapolatory/generalization tasks. | 反映这一新学习理念的一个近期例子是"**物理信息神经网络（PINNs）**"家族^7^。这是一类深度学习算法，能够无缝整合数据与抽象数学算子，包括**物理完整或物理缺失**的 PDE（Box 2、Box 3）。开发这些算法的主要动机在于：这类先验知识或约束可以产生**更具可解释性**的 ML 方法，它们在数据不完美（如数值缺失或带噪、离群点等）时依然稳健，并能给出准确且物理一致的预测——**即便在外推/泛化任务中也是如此**。 |

**三类场景与"中间地带"（P2 第 3 段）**

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Despite numerous public databases, the volume of useful experimental data for complex physical systems is limited. The specific data-driven approach to the predictive modelling of such systems depends crucially on the amount of data available and on the complexity of the system itself, as illustrated in Box 1. | 尽管公共数据库众多，对复杂物理系统而言**有用的实验数据量仍然有限**。如 Box 1 所示，对此类系统做预测建模时，具体采用何种数据驱动方法，**关键取决于可用数据量以及系统本身的复杂度**。 |
| The classical paradigm is shown on the left side of the figure in Box 1, where it is assumed that the only data available are the boundary conditions and initial conditions whereas the specific governing PDEs and associated parameters are precisely known. | **经典范式**见 Box 1 图左侧：假定唯一可用的数据就是边界条件与初始条件，而具体的支配性 PDE 及相关参数是**精确已知**的。 |
| On the other extreme (on the right side of the figure), a lot of data may be available, for instance, in the form of time series, but the governing physical law (the underlying PDE) may not be known at the continuum level^7–9^. | 另一个极端（图右侧）则是：可能有**大量数据**（例如以时间序列形式），但支配性物理定律（底层 PDE）在连续介质层面**并不已知**^7–9^。 |

| It is clear that this middle category is the most general case, and in fact it is representative of the other two categories, if the measurements are too few or too many. | 显然，这个**中间类别是最一般的情形**；事实上，当测量值**太少或太多**时，它就分别退化为另外两个类别。 |

**随机与分数阶 PDE：更复杂的"混合"情形（P2 第 4 段）**

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| This 'mixed' case may lead to much more complex scenarios, where the solution of the PDEs is a stochastic process due to stochastic excitation or an uncertain material property. Hence, stochastic PDEs can be used to represent these stochastic solutions and uncertainties. | 这种"混合"情形可能导致**复杂得多的场景**：由于随机激励或材料属性的不确定性，PDE 的**解本身就是一个随机过程**。因此，可以用**随机 PDE** 来表示这些随机解与不确定性。 |
| Finally, there are many problems involving long-range spatiotemporal interactions, such as turbulence, visco-elasto-plastic materials or other anomalous transport processes, where non-local or fractional calculus and fractional PDEs may be the appropriate mathematical language to adequately describe such phenomena as they exhibit a rich expressivity not unlike that of deep neural networks (DNNs). | 最后，还有大量涉及**长程时空相互作用**的问题，例如湍流、黏弹塑性材料或其他异常输运过程。在这些问题中，**非局部或分数阶微积分与分数阶 PDE** 可能是恰当描述此类现象的数学语言——因为它们展现出与深度神经网络（DNN）不相上下的丰富表达能力。 |

**传统计算的两大现实困境：参数爆炸与代码维护（P2 第 5–6 段）**

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Over the past two decades, efforts to account for uncertainty quantification in computer simulations have led to highly parameterized formulations that may include hundreds of uncertain parameters for complex problems, often rendering such computations infeasible in practice. Typically, computer codes at the national labs and even open-source programs such as OpenFOAM^10^ or LAMMPS^11^ have more than 100,000 lines of code, making it almost impossible to maintain and update them from one generation to the next. | 过去二十年间，为使计算机模拟能够考虑**不确定性量化**，人们提出了高度参数化的表述形式——对复杂问题可能包含**上百个不确定参数**，这往往使得此类计算在实践中不可行。通常，国家实验室的计算机代码、乃至 OpenFOAM^10^ 或 LAMMPS^11^ 这样的开源程序，代码量都超过 **10 万行**，使得它们几乎不可能在代际之间被维护和更新。 |
| We believe that it is possible to overcome these fundamental and practical problems using physics-informed learning, seamlessly integrating data and mathematical models, and implementing them using PINNs or other nonlinear regression-based physics-informed networks (PINs) (Box 2). | 我们相信，借助物理信息学习可以克服这些根本性与实践性难题：无缝整合数据与数学模型，并用 PINNs 或其他基于非线性回归的**物理信息网络（PINs）**来实现（Box 2）。 |

## 本综述的组织 / Structure of this Review

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Making a learning algorithm physics-informed amounts to introducing appropriate observational, inductive or learning biases that can steer the learning process towards identifying physically consistent solutions (see the figure). | **让一个学习算法"物理信息化"，等于引入恰当的观测偏置、归纳偏置或学习偏置，从而把学习过程导向识别出物理一致的解**（见图）。 |
| • Observational biases can be introduced directly through data that embody the underlying physics or carefully crafted data augmentation procedures. Training a machine learning (ML) system on such data allows it to learn functions, vector fields and operators that reflect the physical structure of the data. | • **观测偏置**可以**直接通过数据**引入——这些数据体现了底层物理，或通过精心设计的**数据增强**过程引入。在这样的数据上训练机器学习（ML）系统，能让它学到反映数据物理结构的**函数、向量场与算子**。 |
| • Inductive biases correspond to prior assumptions that can be incorporated by tailored interventions to an ML model architecture, such that the predictions sought are guaranteed to implicitly satisfy a set of given physical laws, typically expressed in the form of certain mathematical constraints. One would argue that this is the most principled way of making a learning algorithm physics-informed, as it allows for the underlying physical constraints to be strictly satisfied. However, such approaches can be limited to accounting for relatively simple symmetry groups (such as translations, permutations, reflections, rotations and so on) that are known a priori, and may often lead to complex implementations that are difficult to scale. | • **归纳偏置**对应于**通过对 ML 模型架构的定制干预**而引入的先验假设，使得所寻求的预测被**保证**隐式满足一组给定物理定律（通常以某些数学约束的形式表述）。人们会认为这是让学习算法物理信息化的**最有原则的方式**，因为它让底层物理约束被**严格**满足。然而，这类方法可能局限于处理**先验已知的相对简单对称群**（如平移、置换、反射、旋转等），而且往往导致难以扩展的复杂实现。 |
| • Learning biases can be introduced by appropriate choice of loss functions, constraints and inference algorithms that can modulate the training phase of an ML model to explicitly favour convergence towards solutions that adhere to the underlying physics. By using and tuning such soft penalty constraints, the underlying physical laws can only be approximately satisfied; however, this provides a very flexible platform for introducing a broad class of physics-based biases that can be expressed in the form of integral, differential or even fractional equations. | • **学习偏置**可以**通过恰当选择损失函数、约束与推理算法**引入，从而调节 ML 模型的训练阶段，明确地**倾向于收敛到遵守底层物理的解**。通过使用并调节这类**软惩罚约束**，底层物理定律**只能被近似满足**；但这提供了一个非常灵活的平台，可以引入一大类以**积分方程、微分方程乃至分数阶方程**形式表达的物理偏置。 |
| These different modes of biasing a learning algorithm towards physically consistent solutions are not mutually exclusive and can be effectively combined to yield a very broad class of hybrid approaches for building physics-informed learning machines. | 这些把学习算法偏向物理一致解的不同模式**并非互斥**，可以有效地组合起来，产生非常广泛的一类构建物理信息学习机的**混合方法**。 |

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| For a specific example, we refer to the recent work in ref.^48^, which proposed new connections between NN architectures and viscosity solutions to certain Hamilton–Jacobi PDEs (HJ-PDEs). The two-layer architecture depicted in fig. 1b defines $f:\mathbb{R}^n\times[0,+\infty)\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ as follows | 作为一个具体例子，我们参照文献^48^ 的近期工作，它在 NN 架构与某些 Hamilton–Jacobi PDE（HJ-PDE）的**粘性解**之间提出了新的联系。图 1b 描绘的两层架构定义了 $f:\mathbb{R}^n\times[0,+\infty)\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ 如下： |
| $$f(x,t):=\min_{i\in\{1,\dots,m\}}\left\{-t\,L\!\left(\frac{x-u_i}{t}\right)+a_i\right\}\tag{1}$$ | $$f(x,t):=\min_{i\in\{1,\dots,m\}}\left\{-t\,L\!\left(\frac{x-u_i}{t}\right)+a_i\right\}\tag{1}$$ |
| which is reminiscent of the celebrated Lax–Oleinik formula. Here, $x$ and $t$ are the spatial and temporal variables, $L$ is a convex and Lipschitz activation function, $a_i\in\mathbb{R}$ and $u_i\in\mathbb{R}^n$ are the NN parameters, and $m$ is the number of neurons. | 这让人想起著名的 **Lax–Oleinik 公式**。这里 $x$ 与 $t$ 是空间与时间变量，$L$ 是**凸且 Lipschitz** 的激活函数，$a_i\in\mathbb{R}$ 与 $u_i\in\mathbb{R}^n$ 是 NN 参数，$m$ 是神经元个数。 |
| It is shown in ref.^48^ that $f$ is the viscosity solution to the following HJ-PDE | 文献^48^ 证明 $f$ 是如下 HJ-PDE 的粘性解： |
| $$\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}(x,t)+H\big(\nabla_x f(x,t),x\big)=0,\quad (x,t)\in\mathbb{R}^n\times(0,+\infty),\qquad f(x,0)=J(x),\ x\in\mathbb{R}^n\tag{2}$$ | $$\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}(x,t)+H\big(\nabla_x f(x,t),x\big)=0,\quad (x,t)\in\mathbb{R}^n\times(0,+\infty),\qquad f(x,0)=J(x),\ x\in\mathbb{R}^n\tag{2}$$ |

## 学习偏置 / Learning bias

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Yet another school of thought approaches the problem of endowing an NN with prior knowledge from a different angle. Instead of designing a specialized architecture that implicitly enforces this knowledge, current efforts aim to impose such constraints in a **soft manner** by appropriately penalizing the loss function of conventional NN approximations. This approach can be viewed as a specific use-case of **multi-task learning**, in which a learning algorithm is simultaneously constrained to fit the observed data, and to yield predictions that approximately satisfy a given set of physical constraints (for example, conservation of mass, momentum, monotonicity and so on). Representative examples include the deep Galerkin method^49^ and PINNs and their variants^7,37,50–52^. The framework of PINNs is further explained in Box 3, as it accurately reflects the key advantages and limitations of enforcing physics via soft penalty constraints. | 又有一个学派从不同角度来处理"给 NN 赋予先验知识"的问题。他们不是设计专门架构来隐式强制这些知识，而是当前的努力旨在**以软的方式**施加这类约束——通过恰当地**惩罚常规 NN 逼近的损失函数**。这种方法可以被看作**多任务学习**的一个具体用例：学习算法被同时约束去拟合观测数据，并给出**近似满足**一组给定物理约束（例如质量守恒、动量守恒、单调性等）的预测。代表性例子包括 **Deep Galerkin 方法**^49^ 以及 **PINNs 及其变体**^7,37,50–52^。PINN 的框架在 Box 3 中进一步说明，因为它准确反映了"通过软惩罚约束来施加物理"这一做法的关键优势与局限。 |
| The **flexibility** of soft penalty constraints allows one to incorporate more general instantiations of domain-specific knowledge into ML models. For example, ref.^53^ presented a statistically constrained generative adversarial network (GAN) by enforcing constraints of covariance from the training data, which results in an improved ML-based emulator to capture the statistics of the training data generated by solving fully resolved PDEs. | 软惩罚约束的**灵活性**允许人们把更一般的领域知识实例融入 ML 模型。例如文献^53^ 提出了一个**统计约束的生成对抗网络（GAN）**，通过施加来自训练数据的**协方差约束**，得到一个改进的 ML 仿真器（emulator），能捕捉"求解**全分辨率** PDE 所生成的训练数据"的统计特性。 |
| Other examples include models tailored to learn contact-induced discontinuities in robotics^54^, physics-informed auto-encoders^55^, which use an additional soft constraint to preserve the **Lyapunov stability**, and InvNet^56^, which is capable of encoding invariances by soft constraints in the loss function. Further extensions include convolutional and recurrent architectures, and probabilistic formulations^51,52,57^. For example, ref.^52^ includes a Bayesian framework that allows for uncertainty quantification of the predicted quantities of interest in complex PDE dynamical systems. | 其他例子包括：为学习机器人中的**接触诱导不连续性**而定制的模型^54^、使用附加软约束来保持 **Lyapunov 稳定性**的**物理信息自编码器**^55^、以及能够通过损失函数中的软约束来编码不变性的 **InvNet**^56^。进一步的扩展包括**卷积与循环架构**，以及**概率形式**^51,52,57^。例如文献^52^ 包含一个**贝叶斯框架**，可对复杂 PDE 动力系统中预测的目标量做**不确定性量化**。 |
| Note that solutions obtained via optimization with such soft penalty constraints and regularization can be viewed as equivalent to the **maximum a-posteriori estimate** of a Bayesian formulation stemming from physics-based likelihood assumptions. Alternatively, a fully Bayesian treatment using Markov chain Monte Carlo methods or variational inference approximations can be used to quantify the uncertainty arising from noisy and gappy data, as discussed below. | 注意，通过这种软惩罚约束与正则化做优化所得到的解，可以被看作**源于基于物理的似然假设的贝叶斯表述的极大后验（MAP）估计**。或者，也可以使用**完全贝叶斯处理**——用马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛（MCMC）方法或变分推断近似——来量化由噪声与**有缺口（gappy）**数据引起的不确定性，如下文所述。 |

## Box 3 ｜ 物理信息神经网络 / Physics-informed neural networks

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)^7^ seamlessly integrate the information from both the measurements and partial differential equations (PDEs) by embedding the PDEs into the loss function of a neural network using automatic differentiation. The PDEs could be integer-order PDEs^7^, integro-differential equations^154^, fractional PDEs^103^ or stochastic PDEs^42,102^. | 物理信息神经网络（PINNs）^7^ 通过**自动微分**把 PDE 嵌入神经网络的**损失函数**，从而把来自**测量数据**和**偏微分方程（PDE）**两方面的信息无缝整合。这些 PDE 可以是**整数阶 PDE**^7^、**积分–微分方程**^154^、**分数阶 PDE**^103^ 或**随机 PDE**^42,102^。 |
| Here, we present the PINN algorithm for solving forward problems using the example of the viscous Burgers' equation | 这里我们以**黏性 Burgers 方程**为例来展示用 PINN 求解正问题的算法： |
| $$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\nu\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$$ | $$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\nu\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$$ |
| with a suitable initial condition and Dirichlet boundary conditions. In the figure, the left (**physics-uninformed**) network represents the surrogate of the PDE solution $u(x,t)$, while the right (**physics-informed**) network describes the PDE residual | 配以适当的初始条件和 Dirichlet 边界条件。图中左侧（**物理未信息**）网络代表 PDE 解 $u(x,t)$ 的代理模型（surrogate），右侧（**物理信息**）网络描述的是 PDE **残差**： |
| $$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\nu\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$$ | $$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\nu\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$$ |
| The loss function includes a supervised loss of data measurements of $u$ from the initial and boundary conditions and an unsupervised loss of PDE: | 损失函数包含一项来自初始/边界条件中 $u$ 的测量数据的**监督损失**，以及一项来自 PDE 的**无监督损失**： |
| $$\mathcal{L}=w_{\text{data}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}+w_{\text{PDE}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{PDE}}\tag{3}$$ | $$\mathcal{L}=w_{\text{data}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}+w_{\text{PDE}}\mathcal{L}_{\text{PDE}}\tag{3}$$ |
| $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}=\frac{1}{N_{\text{data}}}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{data}}}\big|u(x_i,t_i)-u_i\big|^2,\qquad \mathcal{L}_{\text{PDE}}=\frac{1}{N_{\text{PDE}}}\sum_{j=1}^{N_{\text{PDE}}}\Big|\tfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\tfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\nu\tfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}\Big|^2_{(x_j,t_j)}$$ | $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{data}}=\frac{1}{N_{\text{data}}}\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{data}}}\big|u(x_i,t_i)-u_i\big|^2,\qquad \mathcal{L}_{\text{PDE}}=\frac{1}{N_{\text{PDE}}}\sum_{j=1}^{N_{\text{PDE}}}\Big|\tfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}+u\tfrac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\nu\tfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}\Big|^2_{(x_j,t_j)}$$ |
| Here $\{(x_i,t_i)\}$ and $\{(x_j,t_j)\}$ are two sets of points sampled at the initial/boundary locations and in the entire domain, respectively, and $u_i$ are values of $u$ at $(x_i,t_i)$; $w_{\text{data}}$ and $w_{\text{PDE}}$ are the weights used to balance the interplay between the two loss terms. These weights can be user-defined or tuned automatically, and play an important role in improving the trainability of PINNs^76,173^. | 这里 $\{(x_i,t_i)\}$ 与 $\{(x_j,t_j)\}$ 是两组采样点，分别采自初始/边界位置**和**整个域内部；$u_i$ 是 $u$ 在 $(x_i,t_i)$ 处的值；$w_{\text{data}}$ 与 $w_{\text{PDE}}$ 是用来平衡两个损失项相互作用的权重。这些权重可以由用户指定或**自动调节**，并且在提升 PINN 可训练性方面起重要作用^76,173^。 |
| The network is trained by minimizing the loss via gradient-based optimizers, such as Adam^196^ and L-BFGS^206^, until the loss is smaller than a threshold $\varepsilon$. | 网络通过梯度类优化器（如 **Adam**^196^ 和 **L-BFGS**^206^）最小化该损失来训练，直到损失小于某个阈值 $\varepsilon$。 |

## 与核方法的联系 / Connections to kernel methods

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Many of the presented NN-based techniques have a close **asymptotic connection** to kernel methods, which can be exploited to produce new insight and understanding. | More generally, NN methods can be rigorously interpreted as kernel methods in which the underlying **warping kernel** is also learned from data^78,79^. Warping kernels are a special kind of kernels that were initially introduced to model non-stationary spatial structures in geostatistics^80^ and have been also used to interpret residual NN models^27,80^. Furthermore, PINNs can be viewed as solving PDEs in a **reproducing kernel Hilbert space** spanned by a feature map (parametrized by the initial layers of the network), where the latter is also learned from data. | 更一般地，NN 方法可以被严格解释为核方法——其中底层的 **warping kernel（扭曲核）本身也是从数据中学得的**^78,79^。Warping kernel 是一类特殊的核，最初被引入以建模地统计学中的**非平稳空间结构**^80^，也被用来解释残差 NN 模型^27,80^。此外，PINN 可以被看作在由特征映射（由网络初始层参数化）张成的**再生核希尔伯特空间（RKHS）**中求解 PDE，而这个特征映射本身也是从数据中学到的。 |
| Further connections can be made by studying the intimate connection between statistical inference techniques and numerical approximation. Existing works have explored these connections in the context of solving PDEs and inverse problems^81^, **optimal recovery**^82^ and **Bayesian numerical analysis**^83–88^. | 通过研究统计推断技术与数值近似之间的密切联系，还可以建立更多联系。已有工作探讨了求解 PDE 与逆问题^81^、**最优恢复**^82^ 以及**贝叶斯数值分析**^83–88^ 语境下的这些联系。 |
| Connections between kernel methods and NNs can be established even for large and complicated architectures, such as attention-based transformers^89^, whereas **operator-valued kernel methods**^90^ could offer a viable path of analysing and interpreting deep learning tools for learning nonlinear operators. | 核方法与 NN 之间的联系甚至可以建立在注意（attention）类 transformer^89^ 这类大型复杂架构上；而**算子值核（operator-valued kernel）方法**^90^ 可能为分析与解释"学习非线性算子的深度学习工具"提供可行路径。 |
| In summary, analysing NN models through the lens of kernel methods could have considerable benefits, as kernel methods are often interpretable and have strong theoretical foundations, which can subsequently help us to understand when and why deep learning methods may fail or succeed. | 总之，**通过核方法的透镜来分析 NN 模型可能有相当大的好处**，因为核方法通常可解释且理论基础坚实，从而帮助我们理解深度学习方法何时、为何会失败或成功。 |

## 物理信息学习的价值 / Merits of physics-informed learning

**总述：什么场景该用 PINN（P7）**

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| There are already many publications on physics-informed ML across diverse applications in different disciplines. For example, different extensions of PINNs account for **conservation laws**^101^, as well as **stochastic and fractional PDEs** that characterize stochastic phenomena and anomalous transport^42,102,103^. Combining **domain decomposition** with PINNs provides more flexibility for multiscale problems, and, as each subdomain is represented by a different NN, assigned to a different GPU and with minimal communication, its implementation is relatively easy to parallelize^104,105^. | 目前已有许多关于物理信息 ML 的出版物，分布在各个学科的具体应用中。例如，PINN 的不同扩展覆盖了**守恒律**^101^，以及刻画随机现象与异常输运的**随机与分数阶 PDE**^42,102,103^。把**域分解**与 PINN 结合，为多尺度问题提供了更多灵活性；而且由于每个子域可由不同的 NN 表示、被分配到不同的 GPU 且通信开销很小，其形式相对容易并行实现^104,105^。 |
| Collectively, the results of these works suggest that **PINNs are particularly effective for solving ill-posed and inverse problems, whereas for forward well-posed problems that do not require any data assimilation, existing grid-based numerical solvers are currently superior to PINNs.** | 这些工作的结果共同表明：**PINN 在求解病态（ill-posed）问题与逆问题上特别有效；而对于不需要任何数据同化的正向良态问题，现有基于网格的数值求解器目前优于 PINN。** |

### 优势一：不完整的模型与不完美的数据 / Incomplete models and imperfect data

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| As shown in Box 1, physics-informed learning can readily incorporate information from physics together with scattered and noisy data, even when both are imperfect. As recent research shows (ref.^106^), even though the problem is not perfectly well-posed, due to the smoothness/regularity requirements inherent in the PINN formulation, meaningful solutions can be found. Examples include forward and inverse problems with no initial or boundary conditions specified at all, or cases where some parameters of the PDE are unknown — scenarios where classical numerical methods may fail. | 如 Box 1 所示，物理信息学习可以轻易地把**来自物理的信息**与**分散的带噪数据**结合起来，即使两者都不完美。近期研究（文献^106^）表明：即使由于 PINN 表述内在的**光滑性/正则性要求**，问题**并非完美适定**，也能找到有意义的解。例子包括完全没有指定初始或边界条件的正反问题、或 PDE 中某些参数未知的情形——这些场景下经典数值方法可能失效。 |
| In the presence of imperfect models and data, it is beneficial to combine **Bayesian approaches** with physics-informed learning for uncertainty quantification, for example, **Bayesian PINN (B-PINN)**^107^. Also, in contrast to conventional numerical methods, physics-informed learning is **meshless**, requiring no computationally expensive mesh generation and, thus, can readily handle irregular and **moving-domain** problems. Finally, the code is also easier to implement using existing open-source deep learning frameworks, such as TensorFlow and PyTorch. | 在处理不完美模型与数据时，把**贝叶斯方法**与物理信息学习结合以做不确定性量化是有益的，例如**贝叶斯 PINN（B-PINN）**^107^。此外，与传统数值方法相比，物理信息学习是**无网格**的，不需要计算昂贵的网格生成，因此能轻易处理**不规则域与移动域**问题。最后，利用现有的开源深度学习框架（如 TensorFlow 和 PyTorch），代码也更容易实现。 |

### 优势二：小数据区间的强泛化能力 / Strong generalization in the small data regime

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Deep learning usually requires large data to train, but in many physical problems it is difficult to obtain high-quality data of the required accuracy. In these cases, physics-informed learning has the advantage of **strong generalization in the small data regime**. By enforcing or embedding physics, deep learning models are effectively constrained to a **lower-dimensional manifold** and, therefore, can be trained with a small amount of data^58,108^. | 深度学习通常需要大量数据来训练，而在许多物理问题中，很难获得所需精度的高质量数据。在这些情形下，物理信息学习具有**在小数据区间强泛化**的优势。通过强制施加或嵌入物理，深度学习模型被**有效地约束在一个更低维的流形上**，因此可以用少量数据训练^58,108^。 |
| To enforce physics, we can embed physics principles in the network architecture, consider physics as a soft penalty constraint, or use data augmentation, as discussed previously. Also, physics-informed learning has not only **interpolation** but also **extrapolation** capability: that is, spatial extrapolation on boundary-value problems^41^. | 要施加物理，可以把物理原理嵌入网络架构、把物理当作软惩罚约束、或如前所述使用数据增强。此外，物理信息学习不仅具备**插值**能力，还具备**外推**能力：即它能在边值问题上做**空间外推**^41^。 |

### 优势三：用物理理解深度学习 / Understanding deep learning

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| In addition to enhancing the trainability and generalization of ML models, physical principles are also being used to provide theoretical insight and elucidate the inner mechanisms behind the surprising effectiveness of deep learning. For example, in refs^109–112^, the authors use the **jamming transition** of granular media to understand the **double-descent** phenomenon of deep learning in the over-parameterized regime. | 除了提升 ML 模型的可训练性与泛化能力之外，物理原理也被用来提供理论洞见、阐明深度学习惊人有效性背后的内在机制。例如，文献^109–112^ 用颗粒介质的**阻塞（jamming）相变**来理解过参数化区间深度学习的**双下降（double-descent）**现象。 |
| Shallow NNs can also be viewed as **interacting particle systems** and hence can be analysed in the probability measure space with **mean-field theory**, instead of the high-dimensional parameter space. Another work^114^ rigorously constructed an **exact mapping** from the **variational renormalization group** to deep learning architectures based on **restricted Boltzmann machines**. | 浅层 NN 也可以被视为**相互作用粒子系统**，因而可以在概率测度空间里用**平均场理论**分析，而不必在高维参数空间里分析。另一项工作^114^ 严格构造了从**变分重整化群**到基于**受限玻尔兹曼机（RBM）**的深度学习架构之间的**精确映射**。 |
| Inspired by the successful **density matrix renormalization group** algorithm developed in physics, ref.^115^ proposed a framework for applying **quantum-inspired tensor networks** to multi-class supervised learning tasks, leading to considerable computational cost savings. Reference^116^ studied the landscape of deep networks from a **statistical physics** viewpoint, establishing an intuitive connection between NNs and the **spin-glass models**. | 受物理学中成功的**密度矩阵重整化群（DMRG）**算法启发，文献^115^ 提出了一个把**量子启发的张量网络**应用于多类监督学习任务的框架，带来了可观的计算开销节省。文献^116^ 从**统计物理视角**研究了深度网络的损失景观，在 NN 与**自旋玻璃（spin-glass）模型**之间建立了直观联系。 |
| In parallel, information propagation in wide DNNs has been studied based on **dynamical systems theory**^117,118^, providing an analysis of how network initialization determines the propagation of an input signal through the network, hence identifying a set of hyper-parameters and activation functions known as the '**edge of chaos**' that ensure information propagation in deep networks. | 与此并行，宽 DNN 中的**信息传播**已基于**动力系统理论**被研究^117,118^，分析了网络初始化如何决定输入信号在网络中的传播，从而识别出一组超参数与激活函数——被称为"**混沌边缘（edge of chaos）**"——它们能确保深度网络中的信息传播。 |

### 优势四：处理高维问题 / Tackling high dimensionality

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Deep learning is very successful at solving high-dimensional problems, such as high-resolution image classification, language modelling and high-dimensional PDEs. A reason for this success is its ability to **break the curse of dimensionality** as long as the target functions are hierarchical compositions of local functions^119,120^. | 深度学习在求解高维问题（如高分辨率图像分类、语言建模、高维 PDE）上非常成功。这种成功的一个原因是：**只要目标函数是局部函数的层次复合，DNN 就能打破维数灾难**^119,120^。 |
| For instance, ref.^121^ reformulated general high-dimensional parabolic PDEs as **backward stochastic differential equations (BSDEs)** and used DNNs to approximate the gradient of the solution, and then devised the loss based on the discretized stochastic integral and the given terminal condition. In practice, it was used to solve high-dimensional **Black–Scholes**, **Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman** and **Allen–Cahn** equations^121,122^. | 例如文献^121^ 把一般高维抛物型 PDE 重新表述为**倒向随机微分方程（BSDE）**，用 DNN 逼近解的梯度，再基于离散化随机积分与给定终端条件设计损失。实践中，该方法被用于求解高维 **Black–Scholes**、**Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman** 与 **Allen–Cahn** 方程^121,122^。 |
| GANs have been shown to be quite successful in generating samples from high-dimensional distributions in tasks such as image or text generation. As for their application to physical problems, ref.^102^ used GANs to quantify **parametric uncertainty** in high-dimensional stochastic differential equations, and ref.^126^ used GANs to learn parameters in high-dimensional stochastic dynamics. These examples show the capability of GANs to model high-dimensional probability distributions in physical problems. | GAN 已被证明在图像或文本生成等任务中，从高维分布生成样本方面相当成功。至于它们在物理问题上的应用，文献^102^ 用 GAN 量化高维随机微分方程中的**参数化不确定性**，文献^126^ 用 GAN 学习**高维随机动力学中的参数**。这些例子展示了 GAN 在物理问题中建模高维概率分布的能力。 |
| Finally, refs^127,128^ demonstrated that the **deep operator networks (DeepONets)** can tackle the curse of dimensionality associated with the input space even in **operator regression** and its application to PDEs. | 最后，文献^127,128^ 证明：即使在**算子回归**及其对 PDE 的应用中，**深度算子网络（DeepONets）**也能应对与输入空间相关的维数灾难。 |

## 不确定性量化 / Uncertainty quantification

### 三类不确定性来源 / Three sources of uncertainty

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Reliable prediction of the evolution of multiscale and multiphysics systems requires **uncertainty quantification (UQ)**. This problem has received a lot of attention in the past 20 years, where stochastic formulations were added to traditional computational methods to handle uncertainty stemming from, for example, boundary conditions or material properties^123^. | 可靠地预测多尺度与多物理系统的演化需要**不确定性量化（UQ）**。过去 20 年这个问题受到大量关注，人们在传统计算方法上加入了**随机表述**，以处理来自边界条件或材料属性等的不确定性^123^。 |
| For physics-informed learning models, there are at least **three sources of uncertainty**: uncertainty due to **physics**, uncertainty due to **data**, and uncertainty due to the **learning model**. | 对物理信息学习模型而言，**至少有三个不确定性来源**：**物理**带来的不确定性、**数据**带来的不确定性、以及**学习模型**带来的不确定性。 |

### 来源一：物理带来的不确定性 / Uncertainty due to physics

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| The first source refers to **stochastic physical systems**, which are usually described by stochastic PDEs (SPDEs) or stochastic ODEs (SODEs). The **parametric uncertainty** from random parameters belongs to this class. Reference^132^ demonstrated how NNs can be used as **projection functions** of inputs to recover a **low-dimensional nonlinear manifold**, and presented results for an uncertainty propagation problem in an SPDE with an uncertain diffusion coefficient. | 第一个来源指**随机物理系统**，通常由**随机 PDE（SPDE）**或**随机 ODE（SODE）**描述。来自参数随机性的**参数化不确定性**属于这一类。文献^132^ 演示了如何把 NN 用作输入的**投影函数**，以恢复一个**低维非线性流形**，并给出了一个具有不确定扩散系数的 SPDE 中不确定性传播问题的结果。 |
| In the same vein, ref.^133^ used physics-informed loss functions, that is, the **expectation of the energy functional of the PDE over the random variables**, to train a NN that parametrizes the solution (for elliptic SPDEs). Reference^51^ used a conditional convolutional generative model to predict **densities** of solutions, together with physics-informed probabilistic loss functions such that training data are not required to be labelled. | 同一思路下，文献^133^ 使用物理信息损失函数——即 PDE 的**能量泛函在随机变量上的期望**——来训练一个参数化解的 NN（针对椭圆型 SPDE）。文献^51^ 使用条件卷积生成模型预测解的**密度**，配合物理信息概率损失函数，使得训练数据**不需要标签**。 |
| Notably, GANs, as models designed to learn distributions, provide a strong approach to solving high-dimensional stochastic PDEs, and **physics-informed GANs** in refs^102,134^ were among the first such attempts. With data collected from simultaneous readings of multiple stochastic processes from a limited number of sensors, physics-informed GANs were shown to be able to solve a broad range of problems, from forward to inverse problems, using the same framework^103^. In addition, current results show that GANs, when formulated properly, have the capability to deal with the curse of dimensionality of high stochastic dimensions^135^. | 值得注意的是，作为为学习分布而设计的模型，GAN 为求解高维随机 PDE 提供了强有力的途径；文献^102,134^ 中的**物理信息 GAN** 是最早的这类尝试。利用从有限数量传感器同步读取多个随机过程所收集的数据，物理信息 GAN 能够用**同一框架**解决从正问题到反问题的广泛问题^103^。此外，目前的结果显示，若表述得当，GAN 有能力应对高随机维度问题的维数灾难^135^。 |

### 来源二：数据带来的不确定性 / Uncertainty due to data

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| The second source of uncertainty refers in general to **aleatoric uncertainty** from noise in the data and **epistemic uncertainty** from gaps in the data. This type of uncertainty can be well addressed in a **Bayesian framework**. | 第二个不确定性来源一般指来自数据噪声的**偶然不确定性（aleatoric uncertainty）**与来自数据缺口的**认知不确定性（epistemic uncertainty）**。这类不确定性可以在**贝叶斯框架**中很好地处理。 |
| If physics-informed learning models are based on **Gaussian process regression**, then quantifying uncertainty is direct and can be used for **active learning** and **resolution refinement** studies of PDEs, and even for **designing better experiments**^27^. | 如果物理信息学习模型基于**高斯过程回归**，那么量化不确定性就很直接，并可用于 PDE 的**主动学习**与**分辨率细化（resolution refinement）**研究，甚至**设计更好的实验**^27^。 |
| An alternative approach proposed in ref.^107^ is to use **B-PINNs**. They showed that B-PINNs can obtain reasonable uncertainty bounds that are of the same order as the error and increase as the data noise increases; however, **how to set priors for B-PINNs in a systematic way remains an open question**. | 文献^107^ 提出了另一种方法，即使用 **B-PINNs**。作者证明 B-PINN 能给出合理的不确定性界，其量级与误差同阶，并随数据噪声增大而增大；**但如何以系统化方式为 B-PINN 设置先验，仍是一个开放问题。** |

### 来源三：学习模型带来的不确定性 / Uncertainty due to the learning model

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| The third source of uncertainty refers to **limitations of the learning model itself** — that is, for example, the **approximation error, training error and generalization error** of NNs — and is generally **difficult to quantify rigorously**. Reference^137^ used a convolutional encoder–decoder NN to map source terms and domain geometry of PDEs to their solutions as well as the uncertainty, with a probabilistic supervised learning process for training, where the training data are generated from finite element methods. | 第三个不确定性来源指**学习模型自身的局限**——例如 NN 的**逼近误差、训练误差与泛化误差**——并且通常**难以严格量化**。文献^137^ 使用一个卷积编码器–解码器 NN，把 PDE 的**源项与域几何**映射到**解以及不确定性**，训练采用概率监督学习流程，训练数据来自有限元方法。 |
| Notably, ref.^138^ provides the first attempt to quantify **combined uncertainty from learning**, where the **dropout** method of ref.^139^ is used, and **arbitrary polynomial chaos** is used for the physical randomness. For extensions to **time-dependent systems and long-time integration**, see ref.^42^, which uses dynamic and bi-orthogonal mode decomposition of stochastic PDEs to address parametric uncertainty, a useful approach for long-time integration of stochastic systems. | 值得注意的是，文献^138^ 给出了第一个量化**来自学习的综合不确定性**的尝试，它使用文献^139^ 的 **dropout** 方法，并针对物理随机性使用**任意多项式混沌（arbitrary polynomial chaos）**。关于**时间依赖系统与长时间积分**的扩展见文献^42^：它使用随机 PDE 的**动态与双正交模态分解**来处理参数化不确定性，这是随机系统长时间积分的有效方法。 |

### 案例一：浓缩咖啡杯上方的流动 / Flow above an espresso cup

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| In the first example, we discuss how to extract quantitative information from a three-dimensional velocity and pressure field above a cup of espresso. The input data are based on a video of a temperature gradient (fig. 2). This is an example of **hidden fluid mechanics** as introduced in ref.^106^. **This is an ill-posed inverse problem: no boundary conditions and no other information are provided.** | 在第一个例子中，我们讨论如何从一杯浓缩咖啡上方的**三维速度场与压力场**中提取定量信息。输入数据基于一段**温度梯度**的视频（图 2）。这是文献^106^ 所引入的"**隐藏流体力学（hidden fluid mechanics）**"的一个例子。**这是一个病态反问题：没有提供边界条件，也没有提供任何其他信息。** |
| Specifically, the three-dimensional visualization results (which measure density or temperature) obtained with **tomographic background-oriented schlieren (Tomo-BOS)** imaging are used as inputs to a PINN, which seamlessly integrates the visualization data with the governing equations of flow and a passive scalar to infer the **latent quantities**. | The input to the PINN is the **space and time coordinates**, and the outputs are the velocity and pressure fields, trained by minimizing a loss function that includes the **data mismatch of temperature** and the **residuals of the conservation laws of mass, momentum and energy**. | PINN 以**空间与时间坐标**为输入，推断速度场与压力场；它通过最小化一个损失函数来训练，该损失包含**温度的数据失配**以及**质量、动量、能量守恒律的残差**。 |

### 案例五：热力学一致的 PINN（cvPINN）/ Thermodynamically consistent PINNs

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| The physics regularization generally pursued in PINNs admits an interpretation as **least-squares residuals evaluated pointwise** using NN bases. ### 案例六：量子化学 / Application to quantum chemistry

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| In some other applications, researchers use physics to design specific new architectures, together with the principles of physics-informed learning. For example, ref.^32^ proposed the **fermionic neural network (FermiNet)** to solve the many-electron Schrödinger equation from first principles. FermiNet is a hybrid approach with embedded physics: first, to parametrize the **wavefunction**, the NN uses a specialized architecture that respects the **Fermi–Dirac statistics** — that is, it is **anti-symmetric** under exchange of the input electron states and satisfies boundary conditions (decay at infinity). | 在另一些应用中，研究者用物理来设计特定的新架构，并配合物理信息学习的原则。例如文献^32^ 提出了**费米子神经网络（FermiNet）**，用于从头计算（ab initio）求解**多电子薛定谔方程**的解。FermiNet 是嵌入物理的**混合方法**：首先，为参数化**波函数**，NN 采用尊重 **Fermi–Dirac 统计**的专门架构——即它在输入电子态交换下是**反对称**的，并满足边界条件（无穷远处衰减）。 |
| Second, the training of FermiNet is also physics-informed: the loss function is set as the **variational form of the energy expectation value**, whose gradient is estimated using **Monte Carlo methods**. Although the application of NNs eliminates the **basis set extrapolation error**, which is a common source of error in computational quantum chemistry, the performance of NNs depends on many factors in general, including architecture and optimization algorithms, which require further systematic study. | 其次，FermiNet 的训练也是物理信息的：损失函数被设为**能量期望值的变分形式**，其梯度用**蒙特卡洛方法**估计。尽管 NN 的应用消除了**基组外推误差**（这是计算量子化学中的常见误差来源），NN 的性能总体上取决于诸多因素，包括架构与优化算法，这需要进一步的系统研究。 |

### 案例七：材料科学 / Application to material sciences

### 该用哪个模型、框架与算法？ / Which model, framework, algorithm to use?

| The choices **intimately depend on the specific task** that needs to be tackled. ## 当前局限 / Current limitations

### 多尺度与多物理问题 / Multiscale and multiphysics problems

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Despite the recent success of physics-informed learning across a range of applications, **multiscale and multiphysics problems require further developments**. For example, fully connected NNs have difficulty learning **high-frequency functions**, a phenomenon referred to in the literature as the **'F-principle'**^169^ or **'spectral bias'**^170^. Additional work^171,172^ rigorously proved the existence of **frequency bias in DNNs** and derived convergence rates of training as a function of target frequency. | 尽管物理信息学习近期在一系列应用中取得了成功，但**多尺度与多物理问题仍需进一步发展**。例如，全连接 NN 难以学习**高频函数**，这一现象在文献中被称为 **"F 原理"**^169^ 或**"频偏（spectral bias）"**^170^。另有工作^171,172^ 严格证明了 **DNN 中存在频率偏置**，并推导了训练收敛率随目标频率的变化关系。 |
| Moreover, high-frequency features in the target solution generally result in **steep gradients**, and thus PINN models often struggle to penalize accurately the **PDE residuals**^45^. As a consequence, for multiscale problems, the networks struggle to learn high-frequency components and often **may fail to train**^76,173^. | 此外，目标解中的高频特征通常导致**陡峭梯度**，因此 PINN 模型往往难以精确惩罚 **PDE 残差**^45^。其结果是，对多尺度问题，网络难以学到高频分量，且常常**无法训练成功**^76,173^。 |
| To address the challenge of learning high-frequency components, one needs to develop new techniques to aid the network learning, such as **domain decomposition**^105^, **Fourier features**^174^ and **multiscale DNN**^45,175^. However, learning multiphysics simultaneously could be computationally expensive. To address this issue, one may first learn each physics separately and then couple them together. | 为应对学习高频分量的挑战，需要开发辅助网络学习的新技术，例如**域分解**^105^、**傅里叶特征**^174^ 与**多尺度 DNN**^45,175^。然而，同时学习多个物理可能计算代价高昂。为解决这一问题，可以先分别学习每一种物理，再把它们耦合起来。 |
| In the method of **DeepM&M** for the problems of **electro-convection**^60^ and **hypersonics**^61^, several DeepONets were first trained for each field separately and subsequently learned the coupled solutions through either a **parallel or a serial DeepM&M architecture** using supervised learning based on additional data for a specific multiphysics problem. It is also possible to learn the physics at a coarse scale by using the fine-scale simulation data only in small domains^176^. | 在针对**电对流**^60^ 与**高超声速**^61^ 问题的方法 **DeepM&M** 中，先为每个场分别训练若干 DeepONet，随后通过**并行或串行的 DeepM&M 架构**、基于某个特定多物理问题的额外数据进行监督学习，从而学到耦合解。也可以仅在**小域**中利用细尺度模拟数据来学习**粗尺度**上的物理^176^。 |
| Currently in NN-based ML methods, the physics-informed loss functions are mainly defined in a **pointwise** way. Although NNs with such loss functions can be successful in some high-dimensional problems, they **may also fail in some special low-dimensional cases**, such as the **diffusion equation with non-smooth conductivity/permeability**^177^. | 目前在基于 NN 的 ML 方法中，物理信息损失函数主要以**逐点**方式定义。尽管使用这类损失函数的 NN 能在某些高维问题上成功，但在**某些特殊的低维情形下也可能失败**，例如**具有非光滑电导率/渗透率的扩散方程**^177^。 |

### 新算法与计算框架 / New algorithms and computational frameworks

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Physics-informed ML models often involve training **large-scale NNs** with **complicated loss functions**, which generally consist of multiple terms and thus are **highly non-convex optimization problems**^178^. The terms in the loss function may **compete with each other** during training. Consequently, the training process may not be robust and sufficiently stable, and thus **convergence to the global minimum cannot be guaranteed**^179^. | 物理信息 ML 模型常常涉及用**复杂损失函数**训练**大规模 NN**，这些损失函数一般由多个项组成，因而属于**高度非凸的优化问题**^178^。损失函数中的各项在训练过程中可能**相互竞争**。其结果是训练过程可能不够稳健、不够稳定，因此**无法保证收敛到全局最小值**^179^。 |
| To resolve this issue, one needs to develop more robust NN architectures and training algorithms for diverse applications. For example, refs^76,77,173^ have identified two fundamental weaknesses of PINNs, relating **spectral bias**^170^ to a discrepancy in the **convergence rate of different components in a PINN loss function**. The latter is manifested by **training instabilities** leading to **vanishing back-propagated gradients**. As discussed in these refs^76,77,173^, these pathologies can be mitigated by designing appropriate model architectures and new training algorithms for PINNs. | 为解决这一问题，需要针对各类应用开发更稳健的 NN 架构与训练算法。例如，文献^76,77,173^ 指出 PINN 的两个根本弱点，把**频偏**^170^ 与 **PINN 损失函数中不同分量的收敛率差异**联系起来。后者表现为**训练不稳定**，进而导致**反向传播梯度消失**。正如这些文献^76,77,173^ 所讨论的，通过为 PINN 设计恰当的模型架构与新的训练算法可以缓解这些病态。 |
| Also, ref.^104^ used the **weak form of the PDE** and **hp-refinement via decomposition** to enhance the approximation capability of networks. Other examples include **adaptively modifying the activation functions**^180^ or **sampling the data points and the residual evaluation points during training**^181^, which accelerate convergence and improve the performance of physics-informed models. | 此外，文献^104^ 使用 **PDE 的弱形式**与**经由分解的 hp-自适应细化**来增强网络的逼近能力。其他例子包括**自适应地修改激活函数**^180^，或在训练过程中**对数据点与残差评估点进行采样**^181^，这些做法都能加速收敛并改善物理信息模型的性能。 |
| Moreover, the design of effective NN architectures is currently done **empirically** by users, which could be very **time-consuming**. However, emerging **meta-learning** techniques can be used to automate this search^166–168^. What is interesting here is that the architecture may be **changing as the bifurcation parameters of the system (such as the Reynolds number) increase**. | 此外，目前有效 NN 架构的设计由用户凭**经验**完成，这可能非常**耗时**。不过，新兴的**元学习**技术可用于把这一搜索过程自动化^166–168^。有趣的是，随着**系统分岔参数（例如雷诺数）增大**，架构可能需要**随之改变**。 |
| The training and optimization of deep learning models is expensive, and it is crucial to speed up the learning, for instance through **transfer learning via DeepONets** as in the example of **crack propagation** reported in ref.^182^. In addition, **scalable and parallel training algorithms** should be developed by using hardware like **GPUs and tensor processing units**, using both **data-parallel and model-parallel algorithms**. | 深度学习模型的训练与优化代价高昂，加快学习至关重要，例如像文献^182^ 中报告的**裂纹扩展**例子那样，通过**基于 DeepONet 的迁移学习**来加速。此外，应利用 **GPU 与张量处理单元（TPU）**等硬件，同时采用**数据并行与模型并行算法**，开发**可扩展的并行训练算法**。 |
| Unlike classic classification or regression tasks, where the **first-order derivative** is required for gradient descent, physics-informed ML usually involves **higher-order derivatives**. Currently, their efficient evaluation is **not well supported** in popular software frameworks such as TensorFlow and PyTorch. An ML software library that is more efficient for computing high-order derivatives (for example, via **Taylor-mode automatic differentiation**)^183,184^ could greatly reduce the computational cost and boost the application of physics-informed ML across different disciplines. | 与经典的分类或回归任务只需**一阶导数**来执行梯度下降不同，物理信息 ML 通常涉及**高阶导数**。目前，在 TensorFlow 与 PyTorch 等流行软件框架中，对高阶导数的**高效求值支持并不好**。一个能更高效计算高阶导数的 ML 软件库（例如通过**泰勒模式自动微分**）^183,184^ 可以大幅降低计算代价，推动物理信息 ML 在不同学科中的应用。 |
| In addition to integer-order derivatives, other operators such as **integral operators and even fractional-order derivatives**^103^ are very useful in physics-informed learning. | 除整数阶导数外，**积分算子乃至分数阶导数**^103^ 等其他算子也在物理信息学习中非常有用。 |

### 新数学 / New mathematics

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Despite the empirical success of physics-informed learning models, **little is known about the theoretical foundation** of such constrained NNs. A **new theory** is required to rigorously analyse the **capabilities and limitations** of physics-informed learning (for example, the **learning capacity of NNs**). | 尽管物理信息学习模型已在经验上取得成功，但人们对这类受约束 NN 的**理论基础知之甚少**。需要一套**新理论**来严格分析物理信息学习的**能力与局限**（例如 **NN 的学习容量**）。 |
| More specifically, a fundamental question is: **can a network find solutions to PDE via gradient-based optimization?** To answer this question, one should analyse the **total error in deep learning**, which can be decomposed into three types of errors: **approximation error** (can a network approximate a solution to PDE with any accuracy?), **optimization error** (can one attain zero or very small training loss?) and **generalization error** (does smaller training error mean more accurate predicted solution?). It is important to analyse the **well-posedness** of the problem and the **stability and convergence** in terms of these errors. | 更具体地说，一个根本问题是：**网络能否通过基于梯度的优化找到 PDE 的解？** 为回答这一问题，应分析深度学习的**总误差**，它可分解为三类误差：**逼近误差**（网络能否以任意精度逼近 PDE 的解？）、**优化误差**（能否达到零或极小的训练损失？）与**泛化误差**（更小的训练误差是否意味着更准确的预测解？）。重要的是，要针对这些误差分析问题的**适定性**以及**稳定性与收敛性**。 |
| In particular, if the operator to be solved is **(possibly partially) learned by the data themselves**, establishing how **well-posed any problem involving this operator** is becomes an exciting mathematical challenge. The challenge is exacerbated when the **initial/boundary/internal conditions** are provided themselves as (possibly uncertain) **data**. This well-posedness issue must be analysed mathematically, aided by ML computational exploration. | 特别地，如果待求解的算子**由数据本身（可能部分地）学得**，那么确定**任何涉及该算子的问题具有何种适定性**就成了一个令人兴奋的数学挑战。当**初始/边界/内部条件**本身以（可能不确定的）**数据**形式给出时，挑战进一步加剧。这一适定性问题必须在数学上加以分析，并辅以 ML 的计算探索。 |
| The first mathematical analysis for PINNs in solving **forward problems** appeared in ref.^188^, where the **Hölder regularization** was introduced to control generalization error. Specifically, ref.^188^ analysed the second-order linear **elliptic and parabolic type PDEs** and proved the **consistency of results**. References^189,190^ used **quadrature points** in the formulation of the loss and provided an **abstract error estimate** for both forward and inverse problems. However, **no convergence results** were reported, as the use of quadrature points does not quantify the generalization error. | 关于 PINN 求解**正问题**的首个数学分析出现在文献^188^ 中，其中引入 **Hölder 正则化**来控制泛化误差。具体而言，文献^188^ 分析了二阶线性**椭圆型与抛物型 PDE**，并证明了**结果的一致性**。文献^189,190^ 在损失函数的表述中使用**求积点**，并为正问题与反问题提供了**抽象误差估计**。然而，**未报告收敛性结果**，因为使用求积点并不能量化泛化误差。 |
| In subsequent work, ref.^191^ studied **linear PDEs** and proposed an **abstract error estimates framework** for analysing both **PINNs**^7^ and **variational PINNs**^104,192^. Based on the **compactness assumptions** and the **norm equivalence relations**, **sufficient conditions for convergence to the underlying PDE solution** were obtained. The generalization error was handled by the **Rademacher complexity**. For the **continuous loss formulation**, refs^49,193–195^ derived some error estimates based on the continuous loss formulations of PINNs. Although known error bounds involved with **continuous norms** (from PDE literature) may serve as error bounds for (continuous) PINNs, **data samples have to be taken into account to quantify the generalization error**. | 在后续工作中，文献^191^ 研究了**线性 PDE**，并提出了一个**抽象误差估计框架**，用于分析 **PINN**^7^ 与**变分 PINN**^104,192^。基于**紧性假设**与**范数等价关系**，得到了**收敛到 underlying PDE 解的充分条件**。泛化误差则通过 **Rademacher 复杂度**处理。对于**连续损失表述**，文献^49,193–195^ 基于 PINN 的连续损失表述推导了一些误差估计。尽管（来自 PDE 文献的）已知的涉及**连续范数**的误差界可作为（连续）PINN 的误差界，但**必须把数据样本考虑进来才能量化泛化误差**。 |
| In general, NNs are trained by gradient-based optimization methods, and a **new theory should be developed to better understand their training dynamics** (gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent, Adam^196^ and so on). In ref.^197^, **over-parameterized two-layer networks** were analysed, and it was proved that the convergence of gradient descent for **second-order linear PDEs**, but the **boundary conditions were not included** in the analysis. | 一般而言，NN 通过基于梯度的优化方法训练，因此应发展**新理论以更好地理解其训练动力学**（梯度下降、随机梯度下降、Adam^196^ 等）。文献^197^ 分析了**过参数化两层网络**，证明了梯度下降对**二阶线性 PDE** 的收敛性，但分析中**未包含边界条件**。 |
| In ref.^76^, the **neural tangent kernel theory**^198^ was extended to PINNs, and it was shown that the training dynamics of PINNs **sometimes can be regarded as a kernel regression as the width of network goes to infinity**. It is also helpful to understand the training process of networks by **visualizing the landscape of loss function** of different formulations (**strong form, weak form** and so on). Furthermore, more methods are being rapidly developed nowadays, and thus it is also important to understand the **equivalence between models and the equivalence between different loss functions with different norms**. | 文献^76^ 把**神经正切核理论**^198^ 扩展到 PINN，并表明当**网络宽度趋于无穷时，PINN 的训练动力学有时可视为核回归**。通过**可视化不同表述（强形式、弱形式等）下损失函数的景观**来理解网络的训练过程也很有帮助。此外，如今新方法层出不穷，因此理解**模型之间的等价性以及具有不同范数的不同损失函数之间的等价性**同样重要。 |
| Analysing the physics-informed ML models based on rigorous theory calls for a **fruitful synergy between deep learning, optimization, numerical analysis and PDE theory** that not only has the potential to lead to **more robust and effective training algorithms**, but also to **build a solid foundation for this new generation of computational methods**. | 基于严格理论分析物理信息 ML 模型，需要**深度学习、优化、数值分析与 PDE 理论之间富有成效的协同**——这不仅有潜力带来**更稳健、更有效的训练算法**，也能为**这一代新的计算方法奠定坚实基础**。 |

> **专栏术语（原文边栏）**：**Hölder regularization** — A regularization term associated with Hölder constants of differential equations that controls the derivatives of neural networks. ｜ **Hölder 正则化**——一种与微分方程的 Hölder 常数相关的正则化项，用于控制神经网络的导数。
>
> **专栏术语（原文边栏）**：**Rademacher complexity** — A quantity that measures richness of a class of real-valued functions with respect to a probability distribution. ｜ **Rademacher 复杂度**——衡量某一实值函数类相对于某个概率分布的丰富程度的量。

### 未来方向 / Future directions

#### 数字孪生 / Digital twins

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| **'Digital twins'**, a concept first put forth by **General Electric** to describe the digital copy of an engine manufactured in their factories, are now becoming a reality in a number of industries. By **assimilating real measurements to calibrate computational models**, a digital twin aims to **replicate the behaviour of a living or non-living physical entity in silico**. | **"数字孪生"**这一概念最早由**通用电气（General Electric）** 提出，用来描述其工厂制造发动机的数字副本，如今正在多个行业成为现实。通过**同化真实测量来校准计算模型**，数字孪生的目标是在**计算机中（in silico）复现某个有生命或无生命物理实体的行为**。 |
| Before these emerging technologies can be translated into practice, a series of **fundamental questions** need to be addressed. First, **observational data can be scarce and noisy**, are often characterized by **vastly heterogeneous data modalities** (images, time series, lab tests, historical data, clinical records and so on), and may **not be directly available for certain quantities of interest**. | 在这些新兴技术能够转化为实践之前，需要解决一系列**根本问题**。首先，**观测数据可能稀少且含噪**，往往具有**高度异质的数据模态**（图像、时间序列、实验室检测、历史数据、临床记录等），而某些**关心的量可能无法直接获取数据**。 |
| Second, physics-based computational models heavily rely on **tedious pre-processing and calibration procedures** (such as **mesh generation** or calibration of initial and boundary conditions) that typically have a **considerable cost**, hampering their use in **real-time decision-making settings**. Moreover, physical models of many complex natural systems are, at best, **'partially' known as conservation laws**, and **do not provide a closed system of equations** unless appropriate **constitutive laws** are postulated. | 其次，基于物理的计算模型严重依赖**繁琐的预处理与校准流程**（如**网格生成**，或初始与边界条件的校准），这些流程通常代价**可观**，妨碍了它们在**实时决策场景**中的使用。此外，许多复杂自然系统的物理模型**至多只是以守恒律的形式"部分地"已知**，除非假定恰当的**本构律**，否则**无法提供封闭的方程组**。 |
| Thanks to its natural capability of **blending physical models and data** as well as the use of **automatic differentiation that removes the need for mesh generation**, physics-informed learning is well placed to become an **enabling catalyst in the emerging era of digital twins**. | 得益于其**融合物理模型与数据**的天然能力，以及使用**自动微分从而免去网格生成**之需，物理信息学习有望成为**正在兴起的数字孪生时代的赋能催化剂**。 |

#### 数据与模型变换、融合与可解释性 / Data and model transformations, fusion and interpretability

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| As the interactions between physics-based modelling and ML intensify, one will encounter — with increasing frequency — situations in which **different researchers arrive at different data-driven models of the same phenomenon**, even if they use the same training data (or equally informative data, observed through different sensors). | 随着基于物理的建模与 ML 之间的互动不断加强，人们会越来越频繁地遇到这样的情况：**不同研究者对同一现象得到不同的数据驱动模型**——即便他们使用的是相同的训练数据（或通过不同传感器观测到的、信息量相当的数据）。 |
| For example, **two research groups using the same or equivalent alternative data may end up having differently trained networks** (differently learned latent spaces, differently learned operators) even though their predictions are **practically indistinguishable on the training set**. Recognizing that there is often **no unique physical interpretation of an observed phenomenon**, here we foresee the importance of building **ML-based transformations between predictive models, models at different fidelities, and theories**, that are **one-to-one (transformable, 'dual', calibratable) to each other in a verifiable manner**. | 例如，**两个研究组使用相同或等价的替代数据，最终可能得到训练方式不同的网络**（学到不同的潜在空间、不同的算子），尽管它们在**训练集上的预测实际上难以区分**。鉴于对某一观测现象往往**不存在唯一的物理解释**，我们在此预见构建**预测模型之间、不同保真度模型之间、以及理论之间的基于 ML 的变换**的重要性——这些变换应能以**可验证的方式一一对应（可变换、"对偶"、可校准）**。 |
| Researchers are increasingly discovering such transformations (for example **from nonlinear dynamics to the corresponding Koopman model**; **from a Poisson system to the corresponding Hamiltonian one**; **from Nesterov iterations to their corresponding ODEs**) in a **data-driven manner**. Such transformations will allow **data and models to be systematically fused**. | 研究者正越来越多地以**数据驱动的方式**发现这类变换（例如**从非线性动力学到相应的 Koopman 模型**；**从 Poisson 系统到相应的 Hamilton 系统**；**从 Nesterov 迭代到相应的 ODE**）。这类变换将使**数据与模型得以系统地融合**。 |
| **Transformations between the ML latent space features and the physically interpretable observables**, or **ML-learned operators and closed-form equations**, will obviously bolster the **interpretability** of the ML model. Ultimately, one needs to test **how far these transformations generalize**: for what range of observations an ML model can be mapped to a different ML model, or to a physical model, and what the **generalization limit** is, beyond which they **cannot be transformed or calibrated to each other**. | **ML 潜在空间特征与物理可解释可观测量之间的变换**，或 **ML 学得的算子与闭式方程之间的变换**，显然会增强 ML 模型的**可解释性**。最终，需要检验**这些变换能推广多远**：在多大的观测范围内，一个 ML 模型能被映射到另一个 ML 模型或某个物理模型；以及**推广极限**在哪里——超过该极限后它们**无法相互变换或校准**。 |

#### 寻找内禀变量与涌现的有用表征 / Searching for intrinsic variables and emergent, useful representations

| Original text (English) | 中文译文 |
|:---|:---|
| Most of the current physics-informed ML methods follow this paradigm: first define a set of **(humanly interpretable) observables/variables**; then collect data; formulate the physics **completely or incompletely** using a **'reasonable' dictionary of operators** based on the chosen observables; and finally apply the learning algorithm of choice. | 目前大多数物理信息 ML 方法遵循这样的范式：先定义一组**（人类可解释的）可观测量/变量**；再收集数据；基于所选可观测量、用**"合理的"算子字典**把物理**完整或不完整**地表述出来；最后应用所选的学习算法。 |
| An emerging paradigm fuelled by advances in ML is to use **observations and learning methods to automatically determine good/intrinsic variables** and to also **find useful or informative physical model formulations**. Stepping beyond **principal component analysis**, **manifold learning** techniques (from **isoMAP** to **t-SNE** and **diffusion maps**) and their deep learning counterparts of **generative models and (possibly variational) auto-encoders** are used to embed raw observations in **reduced, mathematically useful latent spaces**, in which **evolution rules can be learned**. | 一个由 ML 进展所推动的新兴范式是：利用**观测与学习方法自动确定良好的/内禀的变量**，并**寻找有用或富有信息的物理模型表述**。除**主成分分析**之外，**流形学习**技术（从 **isoMAP** 到 **t-SNE** 与**扩散映射（diffusion maps）**）及其深度学习对应物——**生成模型与（可能是变分的）自编码器**——被用于把原始观测嵌入到**约简的、在数学上有用的潜在空间**中，并在其中**学习演化规则**。 |
| Remarkably, these useful representations can go **beyond embedding the relevant features, the dependent variables in a PDE**. For spatiotemporally disordered data, one can also create **ML-driven emergent spaces**^200,201^ in terms of **ML-learned independent variables**: emergent **'space-times'** in which the model operators will be learned. | 值得注意的是，这些有用表征可以**超越对相关特征（即 PDE 中的因变量）的嵌入**。对于时空无序的数据，还可以用 **ML 学得的自变量**来创建 **ML 驱动的涌现空间**^200,201^：即涌现的**"时空"**，模型的算子将在其中被学习。 |
| The **DARPA Shredder Challenge**^202^ of 2011 recreated space by **effectively solving puzzles**: documents shredded using a variety of paper shredding techniques. Today, disorganized spatiotemporal observations can be embedded in informative **'independent variable' emergent spaces**. For example, evolution operators in the form of **PDEs or SODEs** will then be learned in terms of these new, emergent space — even possibly time — independent variables; there is a **direct analogy here with the discussion of emergent space-time in modern physics**^203^. | 2011 年的 **DARPA 碎纸机挑战赛（Shredder Challenge）**^202^ 通过**有效地求解拼图**重建了空间：那些用各种碎纸技术切碎的文件。如今，杂乱无章的时空观测可以被嵌入到富有信息的**"自变量"涌现空间**中。例如，以 **PDE 或随机 ODE（SODE）** 形式给出的演化算子将按这些新的、涌现的空间（甚至可能是时间）自变量来学习；这里与**现代物理学中关于涌现时空的讨论有着直接的类比**^203^。 |
| Such new paradigms could play a critical role in **design optimization** or in **building a digital twin for complicated systems, even systems of systems**, where humans can hardly write down a neat physical formulation in closed form. Moreover, instead of collecting data from experiments first and then performing the learning algorithm, it becomes important to **integrate both in an active learning framework**. | 这类新范式可在**设计优化**、或**为复杂系统乃至系统的系统（systems of systems）构建数字孪生**中发挥关键作用——在这些场景中，人类几乎无法写出简洁的闭式物理表述。此外，与其先从实验中收集数据、再执行学习算法，更重要的是把两者**整合到一个主动学习框架**中。 |
| In this way, a **judicious selection of new and informative data** can be aided by exploiting the **geometry of latent space** of the learning algorithms, while the algorithms can **gradually improve the choice of latent space descriptors**, as well as the **mathematical formulation governing the physics**, so as to yield **realistic predictions as the experiments go on**. | 这样，可以借助学习算法的**潜在空间几何**来辅助**审慎地选择新的、富含信息的数据**；同时算法能够**逐步改进潜在空间描述符的选择**，以及**支配物理的数学表述**，从而在实验进行过程中给出**贴近现实的预测**。 |
| Ultimately, the main element we see changing is **what we mean by 'understanding'**. Up to now, understanding meant that, say, each term in a PDE had a **physical or mechanistic interpretation** operated on some physically meaningful observables (dependent variables) and also operated in terms of some physically meaningful space/time (independent) variables. Now, it becomes possible to **make accurate predictions without this type of mechanistic understanding** — and **'understanding' is something that may be redefined in the process**. | 最终，我们认为正在改变的核心要素是**我们对"理解"的定义**。迄今为止，理解意味着：比方说，PDE 中的每一项都在某些具有物理意义的可观测量（因变量）上具有**物理或机理上的解释**，并且也是按某些具有物理意义的空间/时间（自变量）来作用的。如今，**在没有这类机理式理解的情况下也能做出准确预测**——而**"理解"本身可能会在这一过程中被重新定义**。 |
